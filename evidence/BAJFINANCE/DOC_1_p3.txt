Bajaj Finance Limited
October 19, 2026
Moderator: Ladies and gentlemen, good day, and welcome to Bajaj Finance Q2 FY27 Earnings
Conference Call. As a reminder, all participant lines will be in the listen-only mode,
and there will be an opportunity for you to ask questions after the presentation
concludes. Should you need assistance during this conference call, please signal
an operator by pressing star then zero on your touchtone phone.
I'm pleased to welcome Mr. Rajeev Jain, Vice Chairman and Managing Director; and
Mr. Sandeep Jain, COO and CFO; and other senior members of the management
team. I now invite Mr. Rajeev Jain for his opening remarks, post which we will open
the floor for the Q&A session. Thank you, and over to you, Mr. Jain.
Rajeev Jain: Thank you for hosting us. Very good evening to all of you, or good morning,
depending on the geography you're in. Welcome to the BFL Q2 earnings call. The
investor deck for Q2 has been uploaded on our website. I hope you had a chance to
go through the same. I'll quickly focus on key updates for the quarter and our
management assessment before Q&A. I expect to between 15 to 20 odd minutes.
Let's quickly jump to Panel 4 in terms of the quarter went by. Overall, I would say it
is an excellent quarter with strong performance across all key metrics, of AUM, new
customer addition, credit cost, profit growth, ROA and ROE. AUM, as you can see,
grew by 26.5% to INR 5,84,624 crores, delivering net AUM addition of close to INR
38,000 crores in the quarter. PBT grew by 29% to INR 8,537 crores in Q2. This is
before the macroeconomic provision that we've created of another INR 337 crores
to grew by 34% to INR 8,874 crores.
PAT grew by 29% to INR6,367 crores. Before macro overlay provision, it grew by
34% to INR6,619 crores. In Q2, company booked just a tad below INR13.5 million
new loans and added 4.5 million new customers. Overall customer franchise stood
at just a tad below 129 million customers.
ROA for the quarter came in at 4.6% and ROE came in at 20.6%. Opex to total income
was 33% and net NPA came in at 42 basis points. Overall, I would say, a pretty good
quarter for the firm and first half for the firm.
In general, AUM growth was pretty secular. I'll quickly go through -- I've jumped
actually to Panel 52. If you see, the growth is pretty secular, urban consumer
finance grew by 43%, rural consumer finance by 63%. MFI grew on a low base, grew
54%, CV and Tractor on low base grew 89%, LAS grew 42%, captive book, as you're
aware, as I've been talking for the last 2 quarters, continues to wind down and
would create significant tailwinds for us in terms of credit costs as you move
forward is now down to just INR2,400-odd crores.
