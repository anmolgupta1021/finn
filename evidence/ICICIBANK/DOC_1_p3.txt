First, moving on to profit and capital
A. Profit and capital
1. Net interest income grew by 17.7% year-on-year to ₹25,339 crore in Q2-2027
2. Net interest margin was 4.18% in Q2-2027 compared to 4.36% in Q1-2027.
Excluding the benefit of interest on tax refund, the net interest margin would have
been 4.28% in Q1-2027
3. Fee income grew by 20.1% year-on-year to ₹7,795 crore in Q2-2027
4. Operating expenses grew by 8.5% year-on-year to ₹12,811 crore in Q2-2027
5. Core operating profit grew by 21.8% year-on-year to ₹20,805 crore in Q2-2027
6. Core operating profit excluding dividend from subsidiaries grew by 25% year-on-
year to ₹20,342 crore in Q2-2027
7. Provisions (excluding provision for tax) were ₹851 crore in Q2-2027
8. Profit before tax excluding treasury grew by 23.4% year-on-year to ₹19,954 crore
in Q2-2027
9. Profit after tax grew by 20.1% year-on-year to ₹14,843 crore in Q2-2027
10. Standalone RoE was 16.6% in Q2-2027
11. At September 30, 2026, the Bank had net worth of about ₹3.6 lakh crore. CET-1
ratio was 16.01%, and total capital adequacy ratio was 16.68% at September 30,
2026
Moving on to deposit growth
B. Deposit growth
1. Total period-end deposits increased by 27.1% year-on-year and 11.8% quarter-
on-quarter at September 30, 2026. The Bank has mobilised gross FCNR (B)
deposits of about $18 billion, pursuant to Reserve Bank of India’s swap facility
2. Average deposits increased by 21.2% year-on-year and 8% sequentially during
Q2-2027
3. Average current and savings account deposits increased by 10.7% year-on-year
4. The Bank opened 145 branches in H1-2027 and had a network of 7,656 branches
and 12,143 ATMs and cash recycling machines at September 30, 2026
Moving on to loan growth
C. Loan growth
1. The total loan portfolio grew by 30% year-on-year and 12.3% quarter on-quarter
at September 30, 2026
2. The retail and rural loan book grew by 14.8% year-on-year. Including non-fund
outstanding, the retail loan portfolio was 43% of the total portfolio. The mortgage
portfolio grew by 15.3% year-on-year. The personal loan portfolio grew by 16.6%
year-on-year. The credit card portfolio declined by 9.7% year-on-year. Jewel loans
grew by 79.4% year-on-year. The business banking portfolio grew by 33.8% year-
on-year. Growth in the domestic corporate portfolio was 30.6% year-on-year at
September 30, 2026
3. About 73.2% of the corporate loan portfolio was rated ‘A- and above’ at
September 30, 2026
