months was up by 40% as compared to the same period last year. Large deal pipeline continues
to be healthy.

at $965 mn, which is 113% of adjusted net profit. Adjusted free cash flow conversion for 9 months
stood at 118%.

crores to our shareholders, which will help EPS accretion. We also paid out interim dividend for
